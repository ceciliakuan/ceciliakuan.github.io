About

I research clinical speech and language AI, focusing on synthetic data generation and clinical transcription for healthcare, with a growing interest in model development and training methods. My current work investigates why LLM-generated Dutch medical dialogues fall short of natural conversation, examining features that might be missed in text-based training. Other work spans Dutch clinical ASR development and LLM evaluation for privacy-preserving clinical text, combining linguistic depth with applied AI and data engineering methods. Open to collaborations and research opportunities in clinical NLP, healthcare AI, or speech and language technology.

Experience

Junior Researcher, Radboud University (2024–present)

  • Design and generate synthetic Dutch doctor–patient dialogues in text using LLMs (structured templates derived from real clinical conversations + one-shot prompting); work accepted at LREC 2026. Current work includes using speaker persona profiles in prompt, and different patient types (age groups, dialects, etc).
  • Build a synthetic speech pipeline converting synthetic dialogues into audio via TTS, including non-verbal vocalization synthesis.
  • Design and build AI pipelines to process and enrich unstructured clinical data (audio and text) for research and data-sharing purposes
  • Developed and benchmarked Dutch clinical ASR workflows (Whisper/WhisperX), improving transcription quality through audio pre-processing, chunking, and error analysis (WER, filler recovery, multilingual handling).
  • Evaluated LLMs (e.g., LLaMA 3) and de-identification tools for privacy-preserving clinical text anonymization.
  • Conducted literature reviews and tool experiments to inform method design and reproducible development.
